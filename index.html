My background

I doubt you will be too surprised to hear that my journey into programming started with video games. In particular - I had the very common starting point of Roblox Studio. Someone who highly inspired me during that time was actually a Unity developer, who goes by the handle "DaniDev" on YouTube. He was funny, and made videos which genuinely entertained even a 10-year-old me, who had the attention span of a cold potato. Not only was I inspired, but my first few projects were mostly based on his games, such as 'Karlson'.

Then, it was time for Computer Science, though not the choppy curriculum taught at the beginning of Secondary School. They started teaching us Python, and naturally, it was surprisingly easy considering my previous experience with Luau. I'd have stuck with Python for a long while, then. I have basically been blinkered on Python and Luau, up until quite recently.

Starting college, all I knew was Python. All of our lessons taught Python. However, they also taught us one thing which changed my entire outlook not just on programming, but also something actually had a tremendous impact on my life. This 'thing' was Linux. I had never used Linux before, and I was very resistant to it at first. SO resistant, in fact, that I used to go out of my way to dismiss it as something people used for no reason at all. I essentially fell for the 'write 50 lines to open google' thing, not guessing it was a joke before. This changed with one tiktok video, which showed a Linux DESKTOP. Yeah, your reaction to that is probably just the same as my current reaction would be to seeing past me shocked by this information. Needless to say - Linux brought me into basically everything I enjoy today. Be it PHP, Bash, databases, wine, desktop ricing, window managers, et cetera. No need to even mention open source development, it became my favourite thing.
